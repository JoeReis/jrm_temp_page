01 / WHY PRACTICAL DATA MEDIA

The tools will change.
The thinking should hold up.

Working with data demands judgment. Knowing which tool to use is only part of it. You need to understand what you’re building, why it matters, and what you’re giving up along the way.

Practical Data Media publishes people with something useful to say, grounded in experience. Our books make room for depth, disagreement, and ideas you can put to work.

Why I started this

I started Practical Data Media to give experienced practitioners a place to share what they’ve learned. The goal is straightforward: publish useful books that help people think clearly and do better work.

Joe Reis · Founder

05 / WRITE WITH US

What book
needs to exist?

You’ve done the work. You’ve learned something worth sharing. Let’s hear it.

We’re interested in original books on data, architecture, modeling, governance, and AI. Bring a clear reader, a real problem, and an argument you can support.

What makes a useful proposal?

Describe who the book is for, what they’ll be able to do after reading it, and what existing books leave unanswered. Include a chapter outline, your relevant experience, and a sample if you have one. Note that we don't work on complete or nearly finished books.
