01 / OUR THESIS

The tools will change.
The thinking should hold up.

Working with data demands judgment. Knowing which tool to use is only part of it. You need to understand what you’re building, why it matters, and what you’re giving up along the way.

Practical Data Media publishes people with something useful to say, grounded in experience. We bring their ideas into books, courses, and conversations that help you think more clearly and do better work.

First principles. Real trade-offs. A point of view.

05 / WRITE WITH US

What book
needs to exist?

You’ve done the work. You’ve learned something worth sharing. Let’s hear it.

We’re interested in original books on data, architecture, modeling, governance, and AI. Bring a clear reader, a real problem, and an argument you can support.

What makes a useful proposal?

Describe who the book is for, what they’ll be able to do after reading it, and what existing books leave unanswered. Include a chapter outline, your relevant experience, and a sample if you have one. Share a read-only document link; a complete manuscript is not required.
